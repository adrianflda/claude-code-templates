## Non-negotiables

1. **Server-rendered HTML.** Content must be in the first HTML response. AI
   crawlers (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, CCBot) do not execute JavaScript.
2. **Animation never reveals content.** Animate `opacity` and `transform` on
   nodes already present in the server HTML. Never gate mounting on scroll.
3. **Motion values come from `design/tokens.css`.** No literal durations or
   easings in components, or reduced-motion and screenshot determinism break.
4. **`lib/site.ts` and `spa-kit.json` stay in sync.** Adding an indexable route
   means adding it to both.

## Editing this site

- Content and layout: `app/page.tsx`. Keep exactly one `<h1>` and at least 300
  characters of visible prose in the raw HTML.
- Brand colours and motion: `design/tokens.css`. Change values there, never in
  components.
- New indexable route: create it under `app/`, then add it to
  `lib/site.ts` (`indexableRoutes`) and to `spa-kit.json`, and add an invariant
  to `contract.json`.
- The decorative WebGL backdrop is `components/shader-field.tsx`. It is
  deliberately deferred to idle time and marked `data-visual-volatile`.
